Refund & Cancellation Policy

Governing milestone escrow deposits, retainer cancellations, and scope adjustments.

1. Advance Milestone Escrow Deposits

Advance milestone escrow deposits (50% Kickoff Advance) are strictly non-refundable once specialist allocation and design/architecture discovery have commenced. Unpaid draft projects may be cancelled freely from the client workspace prior to advance authorization.

2. Monthly Retainer Cancellation

Recurring monthly SEO and maintenance retainers may be cancelled at any time by submitting formal notice through the portal at least seven (7) days prior to the next billing cycle rollover date.
